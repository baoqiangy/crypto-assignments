Chapter 10: Digital Signatures

RSA Digital Signature with Small Integers

Moderate
IndexPrevious ExerciseNext Exercise
Why this exercise fits: Chapter 10 covers RSA digital signatures and the distinction between signing and encryption.

Learning Objectives

  • Sign with a private exponent.
  • Verify with a public exponent.
  • Observe failure after message modification.

Programming Assignment

Using small educational RSA parameters, sign an integer message representative h with s=h^d mod n. Verify by computing s^e mod n. Then modify h by 1 and show that verification fails.

Requirements

  • Read n, e, d, and h.
  • Compute a signature with d.
  • Verify with e.
  • Test the signature against both h and h+1.
  • State in a code comment why real signatures sign a secure hash with standardized padding.

Example / Guidance

With n=3233, e=17, d=2753, h=65, verification recovers 65.
