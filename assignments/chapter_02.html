Chapter 2: Stream Ciphers

LFSR Keystream and Known-Plaintext Demonstration

Moderate
IndexPrevious ExerciseNext Exercise
Why this exercise fits: Chapter 2 covers LFSRs and why a single LFSR is vulnerable when enough keystream becomes known.

Learning Objectives

  • Generate a keystream with a small LFSR.
  • Encrypt/decrypt bits with XOR.
  • Observe how known plaintext reveals keystream bits.

Programming Assignment

Implement an 8-bit LFSR using a supplied feedback rule. Generate a keystream, XOR it with an 8-bit plaintext, decrypt it, and then show that plaintext XOR ciphertext reveals the keystream.

Requirements

  • Use an 8-bit nonzero initial state.
  • Use the feedback bit b7 XOR b5 XOR b4 XOR b3.
  • Generate exactly 8 keystream bits.
  • Encrypt and decrypt using XOR.
  • Print the recovered keystream obtained from plaintext XOR ciphertext.

Example / Guidance

State: 10101101
  Plaintext: 11001010
  Keystream: 10110101
  Ciphertext: 01111111
  Recovered plaintext: 11001010
