Chapter 7: The RSA Cryptosystem

Small-Integer RSA

Moderate
IndexPrevious ExerciseNext Exercise
Why this exercise fits: Chapter 7 is ideal for a complete educational RSA implementation with small integers.

Learning Objectives

  • Generate RSA parameters from p and q.
  • Compute d as a modular inverse.
  • Encrypt and decrypt with fast modular exponentiation.

Programming Assignment

Implement RSA for small prime inputs p and q. Compute n, phi(n), validate e, compute d, encrypt an integer message m, and decrypt it.

Requirements

  • Read p, q, e, and m.
  • Require gcd(e,phi(n))=1 and 0<=m<n.
  • Use fast modular exponentiation.
  • Compute d with the Extended Euclidean Algorithm.
  • Print n, phi(n), d, ciphertext, and recovered plaintext.

Example / Guidance

p=61, q=53, e=17, m=65\nn=3233, phi=3120, d=2753\nciphertext=2790\nrecovered=65
